3. Data Processing Within the Application (Local Processing)

  • No Cookies & No Tracking: This application does not use cookies, nor does it use LocalStorage or SessionStorage for tracking purposes. No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services are integrated.
  • No Server-Side Storage of Your Workout Data: All workout plans, exercises, and labels that you create or edit in the app are processed exclusively in the temporary memory (RAM) of your own browser. When you reload or close the page, all data is completely discarded.
  • File Import and Export: If you use the feature to download (export) or upload (import) your own datasets, this process takes place exclusively locally on your device within your browser. At no point is any workout data transmitted to us or to external servers.
